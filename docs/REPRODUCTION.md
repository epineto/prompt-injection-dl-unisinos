# Reproducing the report's results in the lab

[Versão em português](REPRODUCAO.md) · [README](../README.md)

This guide maps each table, figure and number of Deep Learning Assignment 02 to the lab section (<https://epineto.github.io/prompt-injection-dl-unisinos/>) and gives the expected values. Reference values come from `reference/resultados/` (generated by `reference/python/trabalho2_dl.py`) and are in `data/resultados.json`. Click **EN** in the header for English.

## Tolerances
Browser training uses He initialization and mulberry32 shuffling; PyTorch uses a different initialization and generator. **Deterministic items match** (tokenization, TF-IDF, forward, gradients, one step of each optimizer, the pre-trained model; see `npm test`) and **sampling-dependent items match statistically**: expect values within the seed deviation, not identical ones.

## One-minute shortcut
1. `#testar`: example "Classic injection (EN)" → probability ≈ 91.5% (in Portuguese, "Injeção clássica (PT)": 90.7%; "Safe (EN)": 8.1%).
2. `#treinar`: **Train** with the default values (bilingual, H = 8, takes seconds) → bilingual F1 near 0.88; then "EN only" and "PT only" to see hypothesis H4.
3. `#otimizadores`: **Compare**, with and without "Adam and RMSProp with lr 0.01" → SGD and Momentum reach the minimum first and with lower loss (H1 refuted); with lr 0.01 the adaptive methods get there in 2 to 3 epochs, but with a worse loss.
4. `#backprop`: **Train** → the loss falls and the boundary separates XOR.

## Bilingual data and the translation
**Note on deepset:** the original dataset mixes English and German (and a few other languages, such as Spanish, Croatian and French). The German and other-language texts were **discarded, not converted or translated** (language detection, lexical markers and manual review); 330 English texts remain (269 training and 61 test).

`data/deepset_pt.json` is the aligned Brazilian-Portuguese translation of the full deepset/prompt-injections (546 training and 116 test texts), with `indices_en` (the 269 and 61 positions of the English texts actually used), read by `reference/python/injecao.py` and by the notebook (raw URL of the repository, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/epineto/prompt-injection-dl-unisinos/main/data/deepset_pt.json`). It was made by a language model (LLM) and checked by sampling (about 24 corrections): **not native speech**. It is a derivative work of deepset (Apache-2.0), with attribution. Licenses: deepset and neuralchemy Apache-2.0; xTRam1 declares no license; BR-synthetic (`ai-safety-brazil/prompt-injection-br-synthetic`) CC-BY-4.0, with attribution.

